The complainant Gulzar Ahmed states that Shri Manzoor Khan, a public servant posted as Tehsildar at the Daskroi taluka office, demanded Rs 15,000 from him for releasing his land mutation documents. After negotiation the Tehsildar agreed to accept Rs 10,000. The demand was verified on 12 August 2026 in the presence of two panch witnesses of the Anti-Corruption Bureau.
