On 18 August 2026 at about 9.30 PM the complainant Suresh Kumar Patel parked his motorcycle, registration GJ-01-KL-4455, outside Shreeji Tea Stall near Navrangpura bus stop, Ahmedabad. When he returned after ten minutes the motorcycle was missing. CCTV camera of the adjacent mobile shop shows an unknown man in a black jacket riding it away towards Gujarat University road at 9.42 PM. The motorcycle is valued at Rs 85,000.
